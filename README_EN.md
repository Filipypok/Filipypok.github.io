# LFObdm — Website of “Optom Lanfan” LLC

## 📖 About the Project

Official website of “Optom Lanfan” LLC,  
a company specializing in the import and export of auto parts  
and industrial equipment from China.

🌐 Website: https://lfobdm.ru


## 🚀 Functionality

- Viewing the catalog of auto parts, industrial equipment, and custom-order components
- Company information page
- Contact information
- Request / feedback form
- Responsive layout


## 📂 Main Pages

- **Home** — Company overview, key business areas, advantages of cooperation, and brief information about supplies from China
- **Catalog** — List of auto parts and industrial equipment available for import and wholesale supply
- **About** — Information about “Optom Lanfan” LLC, specialization, experience, and cooperation details
- **Request / Inquiry** — Form for submitting partnership requests or product inquiries
- **Contacts** — Company contact details, communication methods, and feedback form


## 🛠 Technologies Used

- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript
